Original 14-day CBT readiness checklist

Print or copy this list. Tick items as you finish — designed for engineering CTS trainees; non-engineering trainees can swap Drawing/WCS time into Trade Theory.

  • Download / open your latest CTS syllabus PDF from Bharat Skills and highlight unfinished topics
  • Write one formula sheet for Workshop Calculation & Science (units, area, volume, ratios)
  • Revise Employability Skills module titles and make 1-page notes each
  • Complete one full mock (timed 2 hours) and list wrong topics
  • Safety & tools: list 10 PPE / workshop safety points in Hindi + English
  • Drawing: practise reading simple orthographic views and common symbols
  • Trade Theory: revise 5 high-yield chapters with self-made MCQs
  • Sleep and exam logistics: admit card print, ID proof, centre route, leave early

Weekly study rhythm (original template)

  1. Mon–Tue: Trade Theory deep work (90–120 min) + 15 ES MCQs
  2. Wed: WCS problem set (20 Q) + short drawing recall
  3. Thu: Weak-topic repair from Monday mistakes
  4. Fri: Mixed mini-mock (25–30 Q) under time pressure
  5. Sat: Full or half mock + error log
  6. Sun: Light revision + rest; no new heavy chapters
