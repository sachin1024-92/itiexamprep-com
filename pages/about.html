About ITI Exam Prep

Helping ITI trainees prepare smarter for All India Trade Tests — free, clear, and mobile-first.

ITI Exam Prep is an independent study website for trainees preparing for NCVT/SCVT CTS AITT CBT exams. We publish exam pattern explainers, trade hubs, study tips, and free interactive mock quizzes.

Who runs this?

The project is maintained under the Success Tips brand by Sachin Majhee, based in Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh. Contact: sachin1024@gmail.com.

Our principles

  • Free access — no paywalls for core prep content
  • Link to official sources; do not host copyrighted NIMI PDFs
  • Clear disclaimer: we are not DGT/NCVT
  • Practical Hindi+English friendly explanations
