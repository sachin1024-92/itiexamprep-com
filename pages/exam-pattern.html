ITI AITT CBT Exam Pattern 2026

Know exactly how marks are split before you revise. Pattern below reflects the widely followed CTS CBT structure — always confirm for your exam cycle on DGT/NCVT.

Quick facts (CBT Theory)

  • Mode: Computer Based Test (CBT)
  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Question type: Multiple Choice (MCQ)
  • Marks: 2 marks per question
  • Negative marking: None
  • Pass (indicative): Around 33%

Tip: No negative marking means attempt every question — but don’t guess blindly without eliminating options.

Engineering trades (Electrician, Fitter, Electronics Mechanic, MMV, Welder*, Mechanic Diesel, etc.)

Typical paper: 75 questions · 150 marks

Non-engineering trades (COPA and similar)

Typical split: Trade Theory 50 questions + Employability Skills 25 questions (75 Q / 150 marks when both appear in the CBT). Confirm for your trade session.

What else is assessed?

AITT includes formative and practical assessments managed through your ITI. The CBT mainly tests theory knowledge. Keep attendance, sessional marks, and practical readiness in parallel with CBT prep.
