Employability Skills (ES) for ITI CBT

ES carries 25 questions (50 marks) in most AITT CBT papers. Treat it as a high-ROI section — many trainees lose easy marks here.

Why ES matters

With ~one-third of an engineering CBT paper coming from Employability Skills, revising ES can lift your total faster than chasing obscure trade theory corners.

Core topic clusters

  1. Communication skills — listening, speaking, email etiquette, workplace language
  2. IT literacy — computer basics, internet safety, MS Office awareness, digital payments hygiene
  3. Entrepreneurship — business idea, banking basics, GST awareness at intro level
  4. Occupational safety & health — PPE, fire safety, first aid, 5S, environment
  5. Soft skills & values — teamwork, time management, gender sensitivity, constitutional values (as per current ES curriculum)

Study from the latest ES curriculum on Bharat Skills. Wording of modules can update — match your year batch.

Quick revision tips

  • Make one-page summary sheets per module
  • Practise 20 ES MCQs daily in the last two weeks
  • Learn definitions of PPE, fire extinguisher types, and basic computer terms in Hindi + English

Original ES mini examples (for revision)

  • Email etiquette: Clear subject, polite greeting, short body, sign-off with name/trade/ITI — avoid ALL CAPS and rude demands.
  • Teamwork: Listen first, share credit, escalate safety issues immediately rather than hiding mistakes.
  • 5S snapshot: Sort, Set in order, Shine, Standardise, Sustain — a tidy bay reduces accidents and search time.
  • Fire extinguisher recall: Match extinguisher type to fire class (e.g. CO₂ / clean agents often discussed for electrical fires — follow your curriculum chart and workplace rules).
  • Digital payments hygiene: Never share OTP; verify UPI IDs; treat “send money to get prize” messages as scams.

Workshop calculation tips (original examples)

Engineering papers often include a small WCS block. Speed comes from a personal formula sheet.

  1. Units: Convert carefully (mm ↔ cm ↔ m). Write units in every step on rough paper.
  2. Circle: Area = πr²; Circumference = 2πr. Example: r = 7 cm, π = 22/7 → area = 154 cm².
  3. Triangle area: (1/2)×base×height. Example: base 10 cm, height 6 cm → 30 cm².
  4. Percentage: “x% of N” = (x/100)×N. Example: 15% of 240 = 36.
  5. Simple ratio: If 3:5 parts total 160, one part = 160/8 = 20 → parts 60 and 100.
  6. Ohm’s law crossover: Even in WCS-flavoured electrical questions, keep V = IR and P = VI ready.

Make 20 mixed WCS questions from your own notebook this week. Time yourself for 15 minutes — accuracy first, then speed.

7-day ES sprint checklist

  • Day 1: Communication + email dos/don’ts (1 page)
  • Day 2: IT literacy + password/phishing basics
  • Day 3: Entrepreneurship vocabulary (loan, GST awareness intro)
  • Day 4: OSH — PPE, fire, first aid, 5S
  • Day 5: Soft skills & workplace values
  • Day 6: 40 mixed ES MCQs (timed)
  • Day 7: Error-log repair only
