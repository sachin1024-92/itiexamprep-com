How CTS syllabus is organised

Each Craftsman Training Scheme (CTS) trade has Trade Theory, practical competencies, and common subjects such as Employability Skills. Engineering trades also include Workshop Calculation & Science and Engineering Drawing in the CBT mix.

Common papers

  • Employability Skills — communication, IT literacy, entrepreneurship, occupational safety (25 Q for most CBT papers)
  • Workshop Calculation & Science — mensuration, algebra basics, heat, force, electricity basics (engineering)
  • Engineering Drawing — orthographic views, symbols, dimensioning (engineering)
