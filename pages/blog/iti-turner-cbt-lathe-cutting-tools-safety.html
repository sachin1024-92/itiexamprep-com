ITI Turner CBT: Lathe Operations, Cutting Tools & Workshop Safety Checklist

Original Turner CBT map: lathe basics, operation traps, tool geometry, measuring, workholding, workshop safety.

26 Sep 2026 · Turner · ~9 min read

Turner CBT rewards purpose thinking. Confirm syllabus on Bharat Skills.

Unofficial guide. Verify dates on DGT / NCVT MIS. We do not host copyrighted NIMI PDFs.

1. Lathe basics flash cards

  • Headstock: spindle and drive end.
  • Tailstock: support / axial tooling.
  • Carriage / apron: moves the tool.
  • Bed: rigid guiding base.
  • Speeds & feeds: larger diameter → usually lower rpm.
  • Centres: dead vs live centres.

Tip: headstock = drive; tailstock = support.

2. Operations CBT traps

Learn the job of each operation:

  • Facing: flat face square to axis.
  • Turning: reduce OD along length.
  • Parting: cut off from bar stock.
  • Knurling: grip pattern by pressure.
  • Drilling / boring: hole on centre; boring enlarges.
  • Taper turning: conical surface goal.

Trap: knurling ≠ heavy rough turning.

3. Cutting tools & geometry

  • HSS vs carbide: idea-level recognition.
  • Rake: chip flow over the face.
  • Clearance: prevents flank rubbing.
  • Tool on centre: tip at work centre height.
  • Coolants: cool, lubricate, flush chips.
  • Chips: never clear by hand near a live chuck.

Tip: clearance prevents flank rubbing.

4. Measuring & workholding

  • Chucks: 3-jaw round; 4-jaw irregular.
  • Centres / faceplate / mandrel: match method to job.
  • Vernier: quick OD/ID/depth checks.
  • Micrometer: finer accuracy checks.
  • Accuracy: verify with instruments.
  • Safety: never leave chuck key in chuck.

Tip: 3-jaw is not always best for irregular castings.

5. Workshop safety checklist

  • PPE: eye protection, footwear, secure hair/jewellery
  • Chuck key never left in the chuck
  • Keep chuck/gear guards in place
  • No loose clothing near the spindle
  • Stop before clearing chips
  • Isolate before chuck/gear changes

6. Three exemplar MCQs

Practice / exemplar questions — not from any leaked paper.

Q1. Main purpose of the lathe headstock?
A. Only measures diameter
B. Houses spindle and drive that rotate the work
C. Stores cutting fluid permanently
D. Replaces bed and carriage
Answer: B
Q2. Knurling vs heavy rough turning — best correction?
A. Same large metal removal
B. Knurling forms grip pattern by pressure; roughing removes metal
C. Neither done on a lathe
D. Knurling only cools bearings
Answer: B
Q3. Which safety habit pair is correct?
A. Leave chuck key in while running
B. Eye protection, chuck-key out, guards on, isolate before changes
C. Loose sleeves near chuck
D. Clear chips by hand while spinning
Answer: B
