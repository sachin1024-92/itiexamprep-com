ITI Fitter CBT: Measuring Tools, Fits & Safety Checklist

A practical, original revision map for Fitter trainees — least counts, fit types, bench-work logic, and workshop safety that show up often in AITT CBT MCQs.

24 Sep 2026 · Fitter · ~8 min read

Fitter theory CBT rewards clear definitions more than long essays. If you can explain why a tool or fit is chosen, you will handle twisted MCQ options calmly. This checklist is original study advice — not a copy of any NIMI book or coaching PDF. Confirm your batch syllabus on Bharat Skills.

Unofficial guide. Always verify exam pattern and dates on DGT / NCVT MIS / Skill India Digital. We do not host copyrighted NIMI PDFs.

1. Metrology flash cards (memorise these)

  • Steel rule: quick linear measure; typical resolution around 0.5 mm — not for tight tolerances.
  • Vernier caliper: OD, ID, depth. Common metric least count 0.02 mm (some instruments 0.05 mm — read the scale on your tool).
  • Outside micrometer (0–25 mm): typical least count 0.01 mm. Zero error check before trusting a reading.
  • Dial indicator / comparator: shows deviation from a master; great for comparison, not a replacement for understanding absolute size.
  • Surface plate + scribing block: flat datum for marking and inspection layout.
  • Feeler / radius / screw pitch gauges: gap, fillet radius, and thread pitch identification — know the purpose of each.

2. Limits, fits & tolerance — CBT wording traps

Learn the idea first, then names:

  • Clearance fit: shaft always smaller than hole → play / easy assembly / relative motion possible.
  • Interference fit: shaft larger than hole → press / shrink fit; no free play after assembly.
  • Transition fit: may give slight clearance or slight interference depending on actual sizes within tolerance.
  • Tolerance: allowed variation = upper limit − lower limit. Tighter tolerance usually means costlier machining/inspection.
  • Basic size: the nominal size from which limits are derived.

Exam tip: if an option says “shaft always larger” for clearance fit, reject it. Clearance = shaft smaller.

3. Bench-work purpose map

  • Marking: transfer layout from drawing using punch, odd-leg caliper, scriber — accuracy here saves scrap later.
  • Filing: remove small amounts; cross-file for stock removal, draw-file for finer finish along length.
  • Sawing: blade teeth point forward; support work firmly; don’t force a dull blade.
  • Drilling → reaming: drill undersize first when a precise hole is needed, then ream for size and finish.
  • Tapping vs die: tap = internal thread; die = external thread. Match drill size to tap chart (don’t guess).

4. Workshop safety checklist (high-yield)

  • Eye protection for grinding, chipping, and flying chips
  • No loose clothing or jewellery near rotating machines
  • Secure work in vice / clamp — never hold small work by hand under drill
  • Keep floors clear; wipe oil spills — slips cause more injuries than “dramatic” accidents
  • Know nearest fire extinguisher type and first-aid box location
  • Report damaged guards and tools instead of “making do”

5. Two exemplar MCQs (original)

Practice / exemplar questions — not from any leaked paper. Written for learning only.

Q1. A trainee measures a 20 mm shaft with a standard 0–25 mm outside micrometer. The expected least count is usually:
A. 0.1 mm
B. 0.01 mm
C. 0.5 mm
D. 1 mm
Answer: B — Typical metric micrometer least count is 0.01 mm.
Q2. Which statement correctly describes a clearance fit?
A. Shaft diameter is always larger than hole
B. Hole is always larger than shaft (assembly with play)
C. Parts can never be assembled
D. Only used for welding fixtures
Answer: B — Clearance fit keeps the hole larger so the shaft assembles with play.

6. One-week Fitter theory sprint

  • Day 1–2: Measuring tools + least counts + zero error idea
  • Day 3: Limits, fits, tolerance vocabulary
  • Day 4: Bench operations (file, saw, drill, ream, tap)
  • Day 5: Fasteners, keys, couplings identification
  • Day 6: Safety + Employability Skills (don’t skip ES)
  • Day 7: Timed mini-mock + error-notebook repair only
