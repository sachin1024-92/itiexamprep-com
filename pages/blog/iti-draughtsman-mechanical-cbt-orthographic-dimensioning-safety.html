ITI Draughtsman Mechanical CBT: Orthographic Views, Dimensioning & Drawing Office Safety Checklist

A practical, original revision map for Draughtsman Mechanical trainees.

3 Oct 2026 · Draughtsman Mechanical · ~9 min read

Draughtsman Mechanical theory CBT rewards clear purpose thinking. Confirm syllabus on Bharat Skills.

Unofficial guide. Verify exam pattern on DGT / NCVT MIS / Skill India Digital.

1. Drawing basics flash cards

  • Drawing sheet & layout
  • Scale
  • Orthographic projection
  • First-angle vs third-angle
  • Isometric (pictorial idea)
  • Working drawing purpose

Exam tip: title block identifies the drawing.

2. Views & section CBT traps

Learn the job of each view:

  • Front / elevation
  • Top / plan
  • Side / end view
  • Section view
  • Auxiliary / detail ideas
  • View selection habit

Trap: section is not an outer photograph.

3. Line types, dimensioning & symbols

  • Visible outline
  • Hidden line
  • Centre line
  • Dimension & extension lines
  • Dimensioning habit
  • Symbols & notes
  • Tolerance awareness

Exam tip: centre lines mark axes.

4. Instruments & drawing habits

  • Drawing board & T-square
  • Set squares & protractor
  • Compass & dividers
  • Pencils / pens & line weight
  • Scale rule
  • CAD habit

Exam tip: use a scale rule.

5. Drawing office safety checklist

  • PPE
  • Sharp instruments
  • Ergonomics
  • Electrical / cable habits
  • Chemical awareness
  • Fire & housekeeping
