🔧 ITI Fitter

Fitter trainees need strong fundamentals in workshop practice theory, measuring tools, and mechanical basics for the CBT.

Engineering/CTS AITT CBT

Syllabus highlights

  • Hand tools, marking, filing, sawing, drilling and reaming
  • Limits, fits, tolerances and surface finish basics
  • Vernier, micrometer, gauges and inspection
  • Fasteners, keys, couplings and simple machine elements
  • Introduction to welding/sheet metal as per syllabus
  • Maintenance and workshop safety

CBT focus

Diagram-based and definition MCQs are common. Revise fits tables and instrument least counts.

Topic outline (original study map)

  1. Bench work: marking, punching, filing, sawing — purpose of each
  2. Drilling, reaming, tapping — tool selection logic
  3. Limits, fits, tolerances — clearance vs interference idea
  4. Metrology: vernier, micrometer least count, slip gauges role
  5. Fasteners, keys, couplings — identification MCQs
  6. Material basics & heat treatment vocabulary (intro)
  7. Workshop safety & housekeeping (5S mindset)

Practice MCQs (exemplar)

Practice / exemplar questions — not from any leaked paper. Written for learning only.

Q1. Least count of a typical metric micrometer (0–25 mm) is often:
A. 0.1 mm
B. 0.01 mm
C. 0.001 mm
D. 1 mm
Answer: B — Standard micrometer LC is 0.01 mm.
Q2. A clearance fit means:
A. Shaft always larger than hole
B. Shaft always smaller than hole (assembly with play)
C. No assembly possible
D. Only press fit
Answer: B — Clearance fit allows relative movement / easy assembly.
Q3. Odd-leg caliper is mainly used for:
A. Measuring temperature
B. Marking parallel lines from an edge
C. Cutting threads
D. Welding
Answer: B — It marks lines parallel to a reference edge.
Q4. Tap is used to:
A. Cut external threads
B. Cut internal threads
C. Measure hardness
D. Drill large holes only
Answer: B — Taps form internal screw threads.
Q5. Filing across the work at about 45° then reversing is called:
A. Draw filing for finish
B. Broaching
C. Knurling
D. Burnishing only
Answer: A — Draw filing produces a finer finish along the length.
Q6. Which instrument compares dimensions to a standard more than it measures absolute size alone?
A. Steel rule only
B. Comparator / dial indicator setups
C. Chisel
D. Hacksaw
Answer: B — Comparators show deviation from a master.
Q7. PPE for grinding typically includes:
A. Only cotton gloves with no eyewear
B. Eye protection and face shield as required
C. No protection if skilled
D. Loose clothing
Answer: B — Eye/face protection is critical against sparks and particles.
Q8. Surface plate is primarily used as:
A. A cutting tool
B. A reference flat surface for marking/inspection
C. A furnace bed
D. A welding fixture only
Answer: B — It provides a precision flat datum.
