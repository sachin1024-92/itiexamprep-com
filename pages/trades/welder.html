🔥 ITI Welder

Welder theory MCQs often test process selection, defects, electrode coding, and safety — pair practical shop experience with book definitions.

Engineering/CTS AITT CBT

Syllabus highlights

  • SMAW, GMAW, GTAW, oxy-fuel basics
  • Joint types, positions and edge preparation
  • Electrodes, filler metals and polarity
  • Weld defects, inspection and distortion control
  • PPE, fumes, fire and electrical safety
  • Symbols and drawing reading basics

CBT focus

Safety and defect identification questions are high-yield. Revise ES and WCS/Drawing if included for your trade code.

Topic outline (original study map)

  1. Safety: arc eye, fumes, fire watch, PPE for welding

Practice MCQs (exemplar)

Practice / exemplar questions — not from any leaked paper. Written for learning only.

Q1. Arc welding PPE should typically include:
A. Sunglasses only
B. Welding helmet with correct shade filter
C. No eye protection
D. Open footwear
Answer: B — Helmet/filter protects eyes from intense UV/IR.
Q2. Porosity in a weld often relates to:
A. Trapped gas in the weld metal
B. Perfect shielding always
C. Too much polishing only
D. Paint colour
Answer: A — Moisture, contamination, or poor shielding can cause gas pores.
Q3. SMAW is commonly known as:
A. Stick / shielded metal arc welding
B. Only laser welding
C. Ultrasonic cleaning
D. 3D printing
Answer: A — Covered electrode arc welding.
Q4. Oxygen cylinder typically has which colour convention awareness?
A. Follow local/standard cylinder colour codes; never guess
B. Always paint yourself randomly
C. No identification needed
D. Same as LPG always
Answer: A — Identify gases by approved markings/codes — never assume.
Q5. Undercut is:
A. A groove melted into the base metal beside the weld toe
B. A type of electrode coating flavour
C. A measuring tape
D. A grinding wheel grit
Answer: A — It weakens the joint if excessive.
Q6. Before welding galvanized parts, a safety concern is:
A. Zinc fumes — ventilate / follow safe practice
B. They never produce fumes
C. No PPE needed
D. Water only cooling inside lungs
Answer: A — Zinc coatings can produce hazardous fumes.
Q7. Slag should generally be:
A. Left on all finished surfaces forever
B. Removed as required for inspection/next pass
C. Eaten for minerals
D. Painted only
Answer: B — Clean slag for quality and multi-pass welding.
Q8. Fire watch after hot work is important because:
A. Hidden smouldering can ignite later
B. Welding never involves heat
C. Extinguishers are decorative
D. Only for office AC
Answer: A — Remain alert for delayed fires.
