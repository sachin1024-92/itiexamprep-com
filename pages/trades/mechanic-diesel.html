🚛 ITI Mechanic Diesel

Mechanic Diesel focuses on CI engines and systems. Link workshop observations to theory for stronger MCQ recall.

Engineering/CTS AITT CBT

Syllabus highlights

  • Diesel engine construction and working cycles
  • Fuel injection pumps, injectors and filters
  • Lubrication and cooling systems
  • Intake/exhaust and turbo basics
  • Emission control awareness
  • Fault diagnosis and preventive maintenance

CBT focus

Cycle diagrams and component function questions appear frequently. Keep ES revision weekly.

Topic outline (original study map)

  1. IC engine fundamentals: strokes, CI vs SI idea

  4. Air intake, turbo awareness, exhaust basics
  5. Starting systems & batteries care
  6. Emission & maintenance schedules mindset
  7. Workshop safety around running engines and fuels

Practice MCQs (exemplar)

Practice / exemplar questions — not from any leaked paper. Written for learning only.

Q1. In a four-stroke CI engine, fuel typically ignites by:
A. Spark plug only
B. Compression heat (compression ignition)
C. Only battery voltage
D. Solar panel
Answer: B — Diesel engines ignite injected fuel in hot compressed air.
Q2. Engine oil’s main jobs include:
A. Only painting the block
B. Lubrication, cooling contribution, cleaning/suspending particles
C. Replacing coolant forever
D. Increasing octane of diesel
Answer: B — Lubricant protects moving parts and helps manage heat/contaminants.
Q3. A clogged fuel filter can cause:
A. Hard starting / poor performance
B. Higher RAM in computers
C. Better mileage always
D. No effect ever
Answer: A — Restricted fuel flow hurts combustion.
Q4. Coolant in the radiator primarily:
A. Removes excess engine heat
B. Lubricates tyre tread
C. Charges the alternator alone
D. Filters cabin air
Answer: A — Cooling system rejects combustion heat.
Q5. PPE near running engines should consider:
A. Hearing protection and eye protection as needed
B. Loose scarves near belts
C. Open fuel pouring casually
D. No ventilation
Answer: A — Noise, moving parts, and fluids need respect.
Q6. Turbochargers generally use:
A. Exhaust energy to pack more air into the engine
B. Only electric fans on the glove box
C. Steering oil
D. AC refrigerant as fuel
Answer: A — Exhaust-driven turbine compresses intake air.
Q7. Battery terminals should be kept:
A. Corroded and loose
B. Clean and properly tightened
C. Painted with thick tar always
D. Disconnected while driving always
Answer: B — Good connections support starting systems.
Q8. Before working under a vehicle, ensure:
A. Proper jack stands / support — never rely on jack alone
B. Only a brick somewhere
C. Engine at max RPM
D. Fuel tank open for ‘ventilation’ indoors
Answer: A — Secure support prevents crush injuries.
