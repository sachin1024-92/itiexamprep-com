⚡ ITI Electrician

Electrician is one of the most popular engineering CTS trades. CBT covers Trade Theory heavily plus WCS, Engineering Drawing, and Employability Skills.

Engineering/CTS AITT CBT

Syllabus highlights

  • Basic electricity: charge, current, voltage, Ohm’s & Kirchhoff’s laws
  • Wiring systems, cables, earthing and safety devices (MCB, ELCB/RCCB)
  • Batteries, magnets, AC fundamentals, power & power factor
  • Transformers, induction motors, starters and basic maintenance
  • Illumination, measuring instruments (voltmeter, ammeter, megger)
  • Domestic & industrial installation practices (syllabus-aligned)

CBT focus

Prioritise safety, motors, transformers, and wiring regulations-style MCQs. Keep a formula sheet for WCS.

Topic outline (original study map)

  1. Safety first: PPE, earthing, isolation, fire extinguisher classes for electrical fires
  2. DC & AC basics: Ohm, Kirchhoff, series/parallel, power & energy
  3. Magnetic circuits, inductance, capacitance at intro CBT level
  4. Transformers: turns ratio, losses, cooling — conceptual MCQs
  5. Motors & starters: induction motor parts, DOL/star-delta ideas
  6. Wiring & protection: MCB, ELCB/RCCB, cable selection basics
  7. Instruments: multimeter, megger, energy meter reading habits

Practice MCQs (exemplar)

Practice / exemplar questions — not from any leaked paper. Written for learning only.

Q1. SI unit of resistance is:
A. Ampere
B. Ohm
C. Volt
D. Watt
Answer: B — Resistance is measured in ohm (Ω).
Q2. In a series circuit, current is:
A. Different in each resistor
B. Same through all resistors
C. Always zero
D. Only in the first resistor
Answer: B — Series connection shares one current path.
Q3. ELCB/RCCB mainly protects against:
A. Only overload
B. Earth leakage / residual current
C. Low voltage
D. High frequency
Answer: B — It trips when leakage current exceeds its rating.
Q4. Power factor of a pure resistor is:
A. 0
B. 0.5
C. 1
D. Infinity
Answer: C — Voltage and current are in phase; cosφ = 1.
Q5. Megger is used to measure:
A. Current
B. Insulation resistance
C. Frequency
D. Flux
Answer: B — Insulation testers measure high resistance to earth/between conductors.
Q6. Fleming’s left-hand rule is associated with:
A. Motors (force on conductor)
B. Transformers only
C. Batteries
D. Soldering
Answer: A — Left-hand rule relates magnetic field, current, and force in motors.
Q7. A fuse should ideally have:
A. High melting point
B. Low melting point
C. Zero resistance always
D. Infinite current capacity
Answer: B — It must melt quickly on overcurrent.
Q8. Nominal single-phase supply frequency in India is:
A. 40 Hz
B. 50 Hz
C. 60 Hz
D. 100 Hz
Answer: B — Indian AC mains frequency is 50 Hz.
