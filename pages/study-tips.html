Study Tips for ITI AITT CBT

A simple plan beats random YouTube hopping. Use these tactics in the last 4–8 weeks before your CBT.

1. Reverse-engineer the paper

Know the mark split first (exam pattern). For engineering trades, Trade Theory + ES dominate. Don’t ignore the 12 marks from WCS or Drawing — they’re often easier if revised.

2. Weekly rhythm (example)

  • Mon–Wed: Trade theory chapters + 30 MCQs
  • Thu: Employability Skills + WCS/Drawing (eng)
  • Fri: Full timed mini-mock (40–50 Q)
  • Sat: Review wrong answers only
  • Sun: Light revision / Bharat Skills videos

3. CBT strategy on exam day

  1. Skim all questions quickly; flag tough ones
  2. Solve easy Trade Theory & ES first for confidence
  3. Return to calculation/drawing items with remaining time
  4. Since there is no negative marking, fill every answer before submit
  5. Watch the on-screen timer — aim ~1.5 minutes per question average

4. Hindi + English

Many trainees think better in Hindi. Learn key English technical terms so both language versions of MCQs feel familiar.

5. Avoid myths

  • Don’t rely on “leaked papers” or fake result sites
  • Admit card / results: only NCVT MIS / Skill India Digital / your ITI
  • Don’t skip ES because it “looks easy”
