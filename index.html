FAQ

Common questions from ITI trainees

What is the ITI AITT CBT pattern in 2026?
CBT is usually 2 hours, MCQ, 2 marks each, no negative marking. Engineering trades: 75 questions (Trade Theory 38 + WCS 6 + Engg Drawing 6 + Employability Skills 25). Non-engineering: Trade Theory 50 + Employability Skills 25. Verify on DGT/NCVT for your session.
What score do I need to pass?
Roughly 33% is the widely cited pass threshold for AITT CBT theory. Practical/formative assessments have separate norms — check your ITI and NCVT MIS.
Where do I get official syllabus?
Use bharatskills.gov.in and dgt.gov.in. We map topics here but do not host NIMI copyrighted books.
Is this site free / official?
ITI Exam Prep is 100% free and independent — not affiliated with DGT or NCVT. We help you prepare; results and admit cards come only from official portals.
